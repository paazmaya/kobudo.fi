# History of Ryukyu Kobudo

The weapon arts of the Ryukyu Islands evolved through the **Pechin** (scholar-warrior) class. 

## Taira Shinken's Legacy
Much of the modern syllabus comes from **Taira Shinken (1897–1970)**, who documented over 40 kata.

![Placeholder for Taira Shinken](https://placehold.co/800x450/800000/white?text=Historical+Photo+Taira+Shinken)

The history of Ryukyu Kobudo is often traced through the preservation work of Taira Shinken, who systematized many of the kata we practice today.
