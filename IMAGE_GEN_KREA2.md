# Image Generation Prompts — kobudo.fi (Krea 2)

## Shared Style

A richly detailed cartoon illustration inspired by Okinawan and Ryukyu culture, with clean expressive outlines, hand-painted textures and vivid saturated colours. Deep crimson, warm amber, gold, indigo and turquoise create a welcoming, cohesive palette; lantern or natural light brings out wood grain, iron patina, woven fibres, stone and paper. Keep the central subject clear, dignified and serious, with small, gently comical moments confined to the background. When people appear, depict a diverse population from Okinawa and nearby islands, with varied ages, genders, builds, skin tones and individual features; clothing and surroundings suit the period and scene. Clear, natural anatomy and recognisable objects, without watermarks or unrelated text.
