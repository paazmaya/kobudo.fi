**Notes**

- The **Kyōkai index page** groups these under **「空手・琉球古武術」**, explicitly distinguishing them from mainland koryū. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawan_kobud%C5%8D)
- The **Ryukyu Kobujutsu** entry stresses that it uses _eight types of weapons_ and preserves more than forty kata, confirming it as a weapons‑centric Ryukyuan art. [nihonkobudokyoukai](https://www.nihonkobudokyoukai.org/martialarts/062/)
- The **Kingai‑ryū karate & Okinawan kobujutsu** entry lists a very wide range of weapons (bō, jō, kama, oar, hoe, sai, nunchaku, three‑section staff, four‑section staff, iron truncheon, shield, etc.), making it another clearly weapons‑heavy Ryukyu system. [nihonkobudokyoukai](https://www.nihonkobudokyoukai.org/martialarts/066/)
- **Motobu Udun‑di** (Ryukyu royal family art) is described as a palace‑guard style including both empty‑hand and weapon use (swords, especially dual‑sword methods). [nihonkobudokyoukai](https://www.nihonkobudokyoukai.org/martialarts/065/)
- **Okinawa Gōjū‑ryū Bujutsu** is primarily an empty‑hand karate system, but is classed here as a Ryukyuan bujutsu; in practice it also uses traditional training implements and can include ancillary weapons work in some lines. [nihonkobudokyoukai](https://www.nihonkobudokyoukai.org/martialarts/067/)
